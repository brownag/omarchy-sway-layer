# 3.1.1 — Directional pair reorientation and modal resizing

## Goal
Implement and verify H/J/K/L plus arrow-key equivalence, conservative two-window cross-axis reorientation, and `SUPER+r` modal resizing in plain Hyprland and Omarchy Lua modes, without deploying or changing live bindings.

## Analysis findings
- The plain config originally bound H/J/K/L only for focus/movement and had a 20-pixel `SUPER+r` resize submap without arrow aliases. Lua mode originally had H/J/K/L focus, H/J/K/L plus arrows for movement, and direct Ctrl+Super resize chords.
- The installed Hyprland client schema exposes address, workspace, geometry, floating, mapped, hidden, and group fields but not tree-parent information. The helper uses a conservative predicate: exactly two non-floating clients on a workspace, both mapped, visible, ungrouped, with unambiguous perpendicular overlap and separation. Other tiled clients or ambiguous geometry fail closed.
- Read-only inspection of accepted ADRs confirmed the helper boundary and geometry-only probing (ADR-0001/0002) and Lua-expression-only dispatch in Omarchy mode (ADR-0003). Installed `/usr/share/hypr/stubs/hl.meta.lua` documents `hl.define_submap`, `hl.dsp.submap`, and related APIs; a read-only live `hyprctl eval` assertion confirmed the relevant Lua functions exist.
- Authorized disposable live testing on Hyprland 0.56.2 confirmed the Lua dispatch sequence `hl.dsp.layout("togglesplit")` followed by conditional `hl.dsp.window.swap({ direction = ... })` reorients a two-window dwindle pair and places the active window on the requested side in all four cross-axis directions. Exact observations are recorded in `planning/STATE_3.md`; no project config was deployed or live bindings changed.
